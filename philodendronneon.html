Philodendron Neon

Homepage Monstera Deliciosas Resources

One of the easiest and most rewarding houseplants!

There are a variety of species of philodendrons, but this page will specifically focus on the Philodendron Neon.

The Philodendron Neon is characterized by its climbing vines and bright, lime-green leaves.

This plant can flourish in a variety of different environments. From low-light rooms, to bright and indirect sunlight, you can have this plant in a dark office, or in your windowsill.

For this plant, use a pot with good drainage, and water it once a week to every other week. Or when the top inch of the soil is dry. Additionally, you can use any kind of common soil mix for this plant, although many manufacturers make mixes dedicated to houseplants.

How to care for a Philodendron Neon

  • Although this plant can survive in many different environments, differences in sunlight will lead to different looks for the plant, specifically in the leaves.
  • Bright and indirect sunlight will lead to full vines. With very healthy vines, you can even end up with leaves that increase in size the longer the vine gets.
  • In my personal experience, too much sunlight will negatively impact your Philodendron Neon.
  • Too much sun will burn the leaves of your plant. In this scenario, the best thing to do is too move your plant out of direct sun, and cut off the damaged leaves (as they will not repair themselves.)
