Monstera Deliciosa

Homepage Philodendron Neons Resources

One of the most popular houseplants, Monstera Deliciosas are known for their large leaves.

Like philodendrons, there are many different kinds of monsteras. Although, when you think of a monstera, you are probably thinking of a Monstera Delicioisa.

Monstera Deliciosas, also known as the Swiss Cheese Plant, are very popular because they grow to be very large, making them ideal for adding some va-va-voom to your space. They have signature heart-shaped leaves with holes, hence the "swiss cheese."

Additionally, these plants are not too difficult to take care of, although they require more maintenance than a typical philodendron or cactus.

What you need to grow your very own Monstera Deliciosa!

  1. A good soil mix. Not too dense, but something that will retain moisture.
  2. A good-sized pot with good drainage. You don't want a pot that is too small where the roots will be too crowded. Aim for something with 2 inches of extra room, and be prepared to repot your Monstera Deliciosa annually.
  3. Water, duh. Monstera Deliciosa can be prone to overwatering, so make sure to water your plant when it is completely dry. If your monstera is in a large pot, aim to water it every 2 weeks.
  4. Last but not least, as Monstera Deliciosas grow on trees in the wild, your plant will need something to climb on. A moss pole is a must have! This will keep your plant from growing all over the place, keep it looking full, and direct its growth upward.
