Common Houseplants and How to Care For Them

Are you interested in learning more about the wonderful world of houseplants? Look no further! This site is dedicated to helping eager souls dip their toes (or should I say shovels?) into this hobby!

Each page will go over a different houseplant and its specificites. The plants this site will go over are both beginner-friendly and popular. They are the Philodendron Neon and Monstera Deliciosa.

You don't need anything fancy to care for houseplants! The things houseplants require to stay alive are the same as humans!:

  • Water
  • Sunlight
    • Food
    • Energy
    • Like how humans need food to stay alive, plants need water and sunlight to create their food through photosynthesis.
  • Shelter
    • For plants, shelter comes in the form of something to attach to. It depends on the plant. For example, orchids grow on the sides of trees, while daisies grow in soil. For houseplants, shelter is a pot with the appropriate potting material--soil, woodchips, water, rocks, etc. Different plants require different materials and densities. Too dense of soil can cause overwatering and root rot.
